# Open Questions

Nothing here has been decided. "Working assumption" is what the design does until an answer arrives, chosen so that either answer stays cheap. Blocks = the earliest phase that needs an answer.

| Q-21 | **What causes the rare hydration mismatch?** React error 418 appeared in about 0.5% of production page loads in one stress run (3 of 627), on pages combining the page animation with streamed ads, and not in immediately later stress runs (0 of 484 production loads checking every error channel, 0 of a further 1,304 checking only one, and 0 of 325 in development). It still fails roughly one in three full browser test runs, across varied tests, including an account flow with no ads on its final page, so the theory that ads and the page animation cause it is not supported. The cause is not known, and my earlier guess of an image loading failure was wrong. React recovers by rebuilding the page in the browser, so visitors should see at most a brief flicker. Separately, "browser back and forward" and "sections reveal on scroll" fail about 1 run in 8 when repeated alone. A side-by-side run on 2026-11-06 gave 10 of 96 failures on the previous commit and 14 of 96 with the database-backed catalogue, so they were already unreliable, and that sample shows no clear change. One full run on 2026-11-06 also failed the role application journey on a browser 404 for an unidentified file, and it then passed 10 of 10 alone. Tests running alongside were removing photos, which may be the source, but this is not established. On 2026-11-07, one 404 was traced to a moderation link prefetching a just-suspended shop, and fixed, but whether that explains the earlier ones is unknown. Also on 2026-11-07, 10 full runs caught the 418 once (1 in about 1,200 tests), on the first load of `/products?page=1`. Clicking a link before the page was interactive was tested and ruled out: 0 of 60 page loads in each of three modes (no click, late click, early click) under 6x CPU slowdown. The captured server page was not the one that failed, because the test had moved on before it was saved. A future capture must save the page by its address at the moment of the error | Recheck with real data and monitoring before launch | Phase 12 |

## Needed from the project owner, outside the design

- Accounts and keys: Supabase, Cloudinary, Resend, and a verified sending domain.
- Before launch, email needs its own settings: a Biqbaq sending domain (ideally a separate Resend account from the mobile app), a new `RESEND_API_KEY` and `EMAIL_FROM`, `EMAIL_LIVE_SENDING=true`, a blank `EMAIL_TEST_RECIPIENT`, a long random `JOBS_WORKER_SECRET`, and a scheduler calling the worker every minute or so. Set a long random `CONTACT_HASH_SECRET`. In the Supabase dashboard's Auth Hooks, turn on Send Email, pointing at the live `/api/v1/auth/email-hook`, and put its secret in `SEND_EMAIL_HOOK_SECRET`. The real project's setting for confirming email must also be on.
- Confirmation that `biqbaq.com` (used in the PRD's examples) is the production domain.
- Docker, or an equivalent, on the development machine for local Supabase and RLS tests.
